spiralout · mobile · dark

Dark version of the goal-first home, direction 1a: indigo night, one matte frosted card per module, module colour used only as ink.

Goal-first home. The five things with a target lead; the daily log sits underneath.

Top strip, in order: sleep, weight, water, coffee. Sleep and calories burnt are read-only because Health fills them in through Health Auto Export; weight is one number typed each morning; water and coffee are tapped all day.

Each goal has one headline number and one bar against its target. Weight is a target with a date, skills a weekly quota, budget a weekly cap, masters and jobs are pipelines. Tap a header to expand; everything else drops to a one-line row and the open module scrolls to the top.

Every tap still writes through one logEvent(kind, payload); every number is derived from the events list. Days are computed in local time, not UTC, so a 01:30 gaming session lands on the right date in Kathmandu.

Food is in the weekly budget, per your answer. No AI anywhere: entry parsing is regex, the sleep notes are arithmetic over the last 7 or 30 nights. Stack is a PWA on the phone, FastAPI + SQLite behind it, Health Auto Export posting sleep and calories in.

Weight target, skill quota and weekly budget are in Tweaks.
